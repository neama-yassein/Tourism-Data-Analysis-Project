# 🇪🇬 Egypt Tourism Analytics Dashboard

**Tourist Behavior, Spending & Destination Insights** — a Power BI dashboard analyzing tourism patterns across Egypt's major cities.

## 📊 Overview

This project explores tourist arrivals, spending behavior, and destination performance across six major Egyptian cities: **Cairo, Hurghada, Aswan, Luxor, Sharm El Sheikh, and Alexandria**. The dashboard is built as a multi-page Power BI report with interactive filters for season, nationality, gender, and travel company.

## 📁 Dashboard Pages

1. **Overview** – High-level KPIs, tourist arrivals by city, spending by destination, seasonal arrivals, and nationality/gender breakdown.
2. **Spending Analysis** – Average spending by nationality, spending distribution, average spending by season, spending vs. trip duration, and outlier analysis.
3. **Destination Insights** – Tourist arrivals, total revenue, average stay duration, and average daily spending by city, plus a geographic map of arrivals.
4. **Key Insights & Recommendations** – Summarized findings and actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Cairo** attracts the highest number of tourists (1,701 tourists).
- Spending varies significantly by nationality — **the United Kingdom** shows the highest average spending (~$2,850).
- Longer trips correlate with higher total spending (e.g., ~14-day trips average ~$3,200 in spend).
- **Winter** is the peak season for tourist arrivals (~3,120 tourists).
- **Sharm El Sheikh** leads in total revenue and average daily spending despite not having the most arrivals, indicating a higher-value tourist segment.

---

## 🎯 Recommendations

1. **Target High-Value Tourists** – Focus marketing on nationalities with higher average spending (e.g., UK, Denmark, Sweden).
2. **Promote Longer Stays** – Design packages/offers that encourage extended trips.
3. **Strengthen Seasonal Campaigns** – Launch targeted promotions during lower-demand seasons (Spring/Summer/Autumn) to balance year-round demand.

## 📌 Business Impact

Using this tourism data helps stakeholders:
- Optimize marketing spend by targeting high-value nationalities.
- Increase average tourist spending through tailored packages.
- Balance seasonal demand across destinations.
- Improve destination-level strategy using revenue and stay-duration data.
